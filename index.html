WEEK 2 MONDAY LESSON OVERVIEW

In this lesson, I am going to:

  • briefly return to the Writing Process

  • synthesize what we have learned into an Analytical Toolkit 

  • introduce you to some of the first Unit 1 Lens-Based Causal Analysis terms, including lived experiences, lived effects, observations, and questionable phenomena

  • prepare you to start your Inquiry Notebooks as soon as you can; it's our First Step in the Unit 1 Writing Process (percolating, pre-writing, brainstorming, and reading)
