About Your Instructor

Hello — I’m Zuzanna Koziatek, your instructor for ENG-W131: Reading, Writing, and Inquiry. I am passionate about teaching and sharing what I know about the Writing Process and multimodal composition with my students. Likewise, I take pride in being a part of my students' growth as critical thinkers. In a world that is too often strange and puzzling, full of questionable phenomena, I hope my students take what they learn about analysis and apply beyond their college writing. This page gives a brief bio and contact information.
